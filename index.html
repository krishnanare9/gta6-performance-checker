Frequently asked questions

Will PS5 Pro get 60 FPS?

PS5 Pro targets 30 FPS at launch. Its enhancements are PSSR upscaling and exclusive ray-traced shadows.

When is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Rockstar has yet to announce a PC version or release date. The official launch platforms are PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

How much storage does it need?

Budget up to 212 GB on consoles and leave extra free space for updates. The installed size can vary by device and game version.

Is Xbox Series S good enough?

Yes, if you’re comfortable with a 30 FPS target and a softer image. It uses FSR to upscale 720p to 1080p, and testing found more frame-rate dips than on the higher-end consoles.

Will there be a 60 FPS patch later?

Rockstar has announced no 60 FPS patch. Its technology production director cited CPU limits for the game’s simulation on PS5 Pro, so a future patch is uncertain.
