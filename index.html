GTA VI console buying guides

PS5 vs PS5 Pro for GTA VI

Already own a PS5? Keep it if your main reason to upgrade is FPS. Both consoles target 30 FPS. PS5 Pro is worth considering if you care about steadier frame pacing, cleaner detail on a 4K display, and exclusive ray-traced shadows.

Buying new? Compare the total price including a disc drive if you want physical games. The Pro sells the drive separately.

Check launch benchmarks · Check PS5 Pro listings in India

Xbox Series X vs Series S for GTA VI

Choose Series X if GTA VI is the main reason you’re buying. Both target 30 FPS. Launch tests found longer dips on Series S in driving and combat, and it reconstructs a 1080p image from 720p. Series X offers a sharper image and steadier performance.

Series S can still make sense on a tight budget. Compare the price after including an expansion card if your existing library already fills the SSD.

Frequently asked questions

Will PS5 Pro get 60 FPS?

PS5 Pro targets 30 FPS at launch. Its enhancements are PSSR upscaling and exclusive ray-traced shadows.

When is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Rockstar has yet to announce a PC version or release date. The official launch platforms are PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

How much storage does it need?

Budget up to 212 GB on consoles and leave extra free space for updates. The installed size can vary by device and game version.

Is Xbox Series S good enough?

Yes, if you’re comfortable with a 30 FPS target and a softer image. It uses FSR to upscale 720p to 1080p, and testing found more frame-rate dips than on the higher-end consoles.

Will there be a 60 FPS patch later?

Rockstar has announced no 60 FPS patch. Its technology production director cited CPU limits for the game’s simulation on PS5 Pro, so a future patch is uncertain.
